# 12. GENERAL UX PRINCIPLES

The pricing experience should communicate:

**Try Gather free. Pay only when you need more.**

Do not make Free feel artificially crippled.

The Free plan should be genuinely useful.

The $12 Pro plan should feel like a natural upgrade once the user starts getting real value from Gather.

Avoid:

* Fake urgency
* Countdown timers
* Excessive promotional banners
* Complicated pricing
* Annual/monthly toggle for MVP
* Multiple tiers
* Enterprise sales messaging
* Large marketing illustrations

---
